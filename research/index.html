Technical Report: Aerodynamic Limitations of the Diverging Ram Inlet

The feasibility of the Diverging Ram Inlet concept was evaluated via analytical operator composition and 2D inviscid CFD simulations at Mach 5. Following an audit of initial modeling assumptions, this study examines the impact of previously overlooked Prandtl-Meyer expansion fans and geometric scaling. The findings demonstrate severe static pressure degradation, rendering it necessary to explore alternative inlet concepts for the design of unstart-mitigating hypersonic inlet systems.
