Wang Flight Systems Research Division LLC

Senior Engineer - Propulsion (3 Openings)

Primary Duties

  • Iterate hypersonic propulsion architectures via simulation and bench testing.
  • Collaborate with senior engineering peers and external research partners.
  • Author public-facing technical documentation and research papers.
  • Manage the engineering budget and judiciously procure required equipment, materials, and instrumentation.
  • Report directly to leadership.

Schedule & Performance Metric

  • Work Model: Hybrid (mandatory lab testing days; self-managed unstructured days for simulation, planning, and peer communication).
  • Performance Metric: Evaluated strictly on scientific advancements (breakthroughs made and failures eliminated).

Requirements & Preferences

  • Mandatory: 5+ years of senior engineering or research role specializing in high-speed compressible flow.
  • Preferred: Direct experience in Rotating Detonation Engines (RDE), hypersonic gas dynamics, or turbomachinery.

Compensation & IP Rights

  • $150,000 USD base salary.
  • 80% employer-covered health insurance.
  • Planned 401(k) with up to 4% employer match.
  • Planned stock options.
  • Full public authorship rights under our Open Science Mandate.

Application Process

Submit CV, technical portfolio (if available given legacy IP restrictions), and 2 reference contacts via the Contact page.

Important note for references: DO NOT SUBMIT WRITTEN RECOMMENDATION LETTERS!! We use blind reference checks.
