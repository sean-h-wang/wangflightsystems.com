Senior Engineer - Propulsion (2 Openings)

Primary Duties

  • Lead design and hardware iteration, trade studies, and bench testing for high-speed propulsion architectures.
  • Ensure rigorous industry engineering standards, workflows, and compliance.
  • Manage technical project scope and procurement of materials and hardware.
  • Author public-facing technical documentation.
  • Report directly to leadership.

Work Structure

Hybrid model featuring mandatory physical lab days alongside self-managed unstructured days for simulation, planning, and peer communication. Performance is evaluated strictly on scientific progress - breakthroughs made and failures eliminated.

Requirements

  • 5+ years of senior engineering experience specializing in aerospace high-speed compressible flow.
  • Direct experience in Rotating Detonation Engines (RDE), high-speed gas dynamics, or turbomachinery preferred.

Compensation

  • $150,000 USD base salary.
  • 75% employer-covered family health insurance.
  • Planned 401(k) with up to 3% employer match and planned stock options.
  • Full public authorship rights under our Open Science Mandate.

Engineering Intern (4 Openings)

Primary Duties

  • Serve as technical assistance assigned directly to a core engineering staff member, doing whatever they ask you to do.
  • Tasks may include hands-on machining, fabrication, experimental test setup, coding for data analysis, and general operational maintenance of lab facilities.

Work Structure

  • Strictly in-person, part-time on lab days.
  • Operates in an execution-focused industrial environment.
  • Signed to 12-week at-will employment terms.
  • Assigned to core staff and begin work on day 1 to a professional standard, with no icebreakers, team-building activities, opening/closing remarks, final presentation symposiums, or other academia crossovers.

Requirements

  • Current enrollment or recent degree in engineering.
  • Demonstrate capability in real-stakes applied technical work and a willingness to learn fast and hold themselves to professional standards.
  • Applicants must be proficient in undergraduate-level coding, linear algebra, calculus III, and classical physics and thermodynamics.
  • Only one reference contact is required for application.

Compensation

  • $7.50 USD per hour compensation.
  • Full public authorship rights under our Open Science Mandate.
  • Direct mentorship from senior technical staff and immersive exposure to high-speed propulsion development.
  • Note: Health insurance, 401(k), and stock options are not included for intern roles.
