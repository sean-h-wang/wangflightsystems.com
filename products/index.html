Miniliner

Our intermediate goal is subscale regional (in size, not range) airframes engineered to field-test our aerodynamic research, validate hydrogen propulsion architectures, and fly the world's first commercial hypersonic flights with 60 passengers.

Wangliner Narrowbody

The next step is scaling to narrowbody aircraft with 200 passengers. Here, we continue to improve flight quality, maintainence reliability, and unit economics, so that hypersonic flight is available to more and more people.

Wangliner Widebody

The flagship objective of Wang Flight Systems. Designed for up to 400 passengers, the Widebody represents the ultimate democratization of high-speed aerospace: turning international long-haul flights into ordinary day trips for everyday people.

A Note on Demonstrators

We don't like demonstrators, and we do not build them just for the sake of it.

Legacy aerospace primes and unserious startups treat demonstrators as an end goal - their target is merely to demonstrate hypersonic flight to satisfy a defense contract (which we will never sign). Our target is to democratize it. While subscale flight testing is an engineering necessity, every airframe we build is built strictly for real-world testing with direct commercial purpose, not to live on a slide deck.
