History

For generations, engineers have pushed the boundary of transportation technology to preserve humanity’s most valuable resource: time. International travel went from an absolute impossibility to a 24-hour stint, unlocking global connections and shared progress.

But somewhere along the line, we stopped. Our best international transportation is subsonic commercial aviation at Mach 0.85, and it has been that way for the past seventy years. We had Concorde for 27 years, but now that’s gone.

Every day, passengers look out of the airplane window, and see all the other sectors advance forward. Safety issues, flight delays, and lost opportunities are tolerated just because there is no better option.

As a Public Benefit Corporation with four subsidiary LLCs, Wang Flight Systems commits to the advancement of open-science civilian aerospace technology. We are building a hypersonic widebody civilian transport, for you to fly wherever you want, whenever you want, at a speed that matches, not delays your timeline.

We are not building for a contract deliverable. We are building for life, so that hypersonic flight is safe, reliable, and affordable for everyday customers. More importantly, every step is publicly published for you to follow along.

Because, flying fast shouldn't be a military privilege or executive luxury. It is a fundamental right of humanity. Hypersonic technology will cultivate opportunity, family, connection, and progress. And we are giving it to you.

Corporate Structure

Wang Flight Systems is not one, but five entities. Each one handles a stage of the aircraft design lifecycle.

Wang Flight Systems Inc.

The Public Benefit parent company holding the brand, strategic vision, and governance. Not yet incorporated.

Wang Flight Systems Research Division LLC

Conducts R&D and engineering services while publicly publishing all technical findings. Incorporated.

Wang Flight Systems Manufacturing Division LLC

Scales prototypes to mass production and handles direct fleet sales to B2B customers. Not yet incorporated.

Wang Flight Systems Certification Division LLC

Provides compliance and certification services while engaging FAA regulators. Not yet incorporated.

Wang Flight Systems MRO Division LLC

Manages long-term unit maintenance, repair, and overhaul operations over the entire service life. Not yet incorporated.
