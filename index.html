Hypersonic Flight for the People

Commercial aviation has been stagnant at Mach 0.85 for seventy years. Wang Flight Systems is changing that by bringing hypersonic technology out of military isolation and directly to commercial travel.

Wang Flight Systems is building the Wangliner™ - the world's first Hypersonic Widebody Civilian Transport.

The Wangliner will be hydrogen powered, FAA certified, and built for ordinary passengers. Anyone can fly long-haul in under 3 hours.

It's time to stop demonstrating. It's time to start democratizing.

Our Design Philosophy

  • Simplicity: We design from first principles with respect to fundamental physics - eliminating brittle workarounds and unnecessary electromechanical patchwork.
  • Reliability: We focus on making aircraft work repeatedly and predictably with sound unit economics - not chasing theoretical novelties or complexity for the sake of it.
  • Certifiability: There is no such thing as acceptable risk. Every single part is engineered for FAA certification from Day 1 to get passengers where they need to be safely.

Open Science Commitment

The science of hypersonic flight belongs to humanity, not locked in vaults and patents. Every Wang Flight Systems finding is published open-access for the world to see and build on.

Civilian Mandate

We believe hypersonic technology should connect people, not deliver weapons. Wang Flight Systems rejects all defense funding, ITAR restrictions, and military contracts of any kind.
