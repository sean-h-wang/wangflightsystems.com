Wang Flight Systems engages with targeted university research partners for a shared scientific effort to advance the fundamental physics of hypersonic civilian flight.

Benefits

  • Full coverage of raw material and hardware costs
  • Direct integration and close collaboration with Wang Flight Systems team members

Requirements

  • No patents, exclusivity licenses, or publication delays (both ways)

  • Alignment towards practical physics to support long term civilian transport goals
